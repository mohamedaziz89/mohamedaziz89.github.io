Flagship case study

Site Speed → Conversion Impact

Blending GA4 conversion, Core Web Vitals, and app-funnel data into one weekly view of where — and roughly how much — revenue leaks in the buying journey.

GA4 · Core Web Vitals (CrUX) · App Funnel (APM) · Python · Google Sheets automation

Finding the revenue leaks between traffic and purchase

2 stages
Login & Checkout — the dominant app drop-offs (≈⅔ lost at each)
"Poor" LCP
mobile load ~2–3× above Google's threshold
3 → 1
three siloed tools unified into one auto-syncing weekly tracker
Problem
Leadership could see conversion was underperforming but not why or what it cost. Conversion, site speed, and app-funnel data lived in three tools that never talked to each other.
Approach
Built one weekly tracker blending GA4 (sessions → view → cart → checkout → purchase), CrUX Core Web Vitals (LCP/INP/CLS/TTFB), and app APM funnel data by stage and platform — with a 16-week vs. prior-16-week comparison and automated refresh.
Result
Identified the app Login and Checkout stages as the biggest leaks (≈two-thirds of ready-to-buy users lost at each) and mobile site speed as a conversion drag; showed the weeks with worst speed lined up with weakest checkout completion.
Decision
Delivered a revenue-ranked fix list to engineering and product, sequenced by expected conversion upside, plus a weekly monitor to measure each fix as it shipped.
